These days it's rec volleyball, pickleball, soccer, weightlifting, and a growing interest in longevity. In past lives: a decade of rock climbing, scuba diving, some silly videos, and a multi-year cold shower phase. Always: reading, trying new food, and failing to learn an instrument.

Current obsession

Longevity — the Bean Boy Order of Operations

My wife lovingly calls me "Bean Boy" because of my proclivity for beans. It stuck hard enough that I wrote a whole framework around it: SLEEPS — Sleep, Love, Exercise, Engage your brain, Plants, Stop health detractors — plus an order of operations for healthy living.

  1. Stop doing things known to be bad
  2. Eat diverse whole food plants
  3. Move frequently
  4. Sleep well
